# Generated Art Manifest

These replaceable raster assets were produced with the built-in GPT image-generation tool for the prototype art pass. Game rules, labels, slot state, and accessibility information remain code-rendered.

## Board

### `board/shattered-realms-map-v1.png`

Wide painterly high-fantasy realm map from an elevated tabletop viewpoint. Twelve landmarks follow the interactive 4×3 grid: crystal cavern, mage tower, ancient library, sacred shrine; dwarven mines, forge hall, market, harbor; wildwood grove, ruined stronghold, dragon pass, and watchtower. Deep sapphire, emerald, stone, gold, and ember palette. No text, UI, dice, cards, logos, or watermark.

## Faction portraits

All four portraits use a matching square bust composition, painterly realism, dark vignette, strong small-scale facial readability, and no embedded UI or text.

- `factions/arcanum-conclave-v1.png`: silver-haired elven archmage-scholar in midnight blue, silver, sapphire, and violet.
- `factions/ember-dominion-v1.png`: dragon-blooded warrior-commander in crimson, coal, bronze, and ember light.
- `factions/verdant-covenant-v1.png`: ancient forest guardian in deep green, warm earth, muted gold, and teal magic.
- `factions/stonebound-league-v1.png`: dwarven master engineer and smith in charcoal steel, leather, brass, amber, and forge light.

## Shared generation constraints

Original character and environment designs; premium fantasy board-game atmosphere; readable at browser-game scale; no protected characters or settings; no text, letters, numbers, logos, dice, frames, or watermarks baked into portraits; no rules or labels baked into the map.
